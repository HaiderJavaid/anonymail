# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Free validation

Ship one local inbox, right-click autofill, side panel/dashboard, unread badge, expiry, privacy page, and developer-mode ZIP.

## Phase 2 — Owned infrastructure

Move to an owned domain with Cloudflare Email Routing, Worker API, D1 metadata, R2 message storage, server-enforced expiry, authentication, rate limits, and abuse controls.

## Phase 3 — Freemium

Keep one short-lived local inbox free. Add paid multiple inboxes, longer retention, persistent aliases, sync, search, forwarding, and custom names.

## Phase 4 — Pro and teams

Add custom domains, QA workflows, webhooks, shared inboxes, and audit controls. Consider replies only after outbound-abuse and deliverability systems exist.
